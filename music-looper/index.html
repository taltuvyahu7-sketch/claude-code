Loop Station

Record loops, layer tracks, shape them with filters & effects, jam over drums.
Press a track’s Rec button (or keys 1–4) to record your first loop. Use headphones to avoid feedback!
Drum machine 16 steps per bar — pattern repeats every bar of the loop.
How it works: hit Rec on a track — after the count-in it records exactly one loop (BPM × loop length) and immediately starts looping. Record the other tracks on top; everything stays in sync. Shape each track with pan, drive, filter (low/high/band-pass or notch), delay, reverb, chorus and tremolo; flip it backwards with ⇆ or switch its speed to ½×/2× (stays in sync). Add a beat with the drum machine, then Export WAV to download the mix.
Shortcuts: Space play/stop   1–4 record track   M metronome.
Tips: use headphones (otherwise the mic re-records your speakers). If your recorded loops feel late against the drums, raise Latency comp; if early, lower it. BPM and loop length lock once a loop exists — Clear all to change them.
